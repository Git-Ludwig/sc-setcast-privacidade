Privacy Policy

SC-SetCast by PJR (camera app and Remote app)
Last updated: 11 October 2026 · Ler em português

This policy explains what personal data the SC-SetCast and SC-SetCast Remote apps (together, "the app") process, why, for how long, and what rights you have over it. The app is designed to record and live-stream volleyball games with a scoreboard overlaid on the video.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is the app's developer, Paulo (PJR), based in Portugal.
Contact for any privacy question: pjr@ik.me

2. Summary

  • Recordings, photos, teams, music and settings stay on your phone. They are not sent to us.
  • When you stream, the video goes directly from your phone to YouTube, to the channel whose stream key you entered. It does not pass through our servers.
  • To manage licences we store your email address, an identifier of the devices where you use the app, and the licence dates.
  • To fix bugs, the app sends us technical usage logs (which screens and options were used and which errors occurred), with no images, audio or stream keys.
  • We do not sell data, do not show third-party advertising and do not use data to build commercial profiles.

The Remote app does not require sign-in and sends no data to the internet. It only communicates with the camera app, over Bluetooth or the local Wi-Fi network.

4. Android permissions

  • Camera and microphone: to record and stream. Used only while the app's camera is active.
  • Storage / media files: to save recordings and to read logos, photos and music you choose.
  • Bluetooth and nearby devices: to connect to the remote.
  • Network and Wi-Fi: to stream, check the licence, connect to the remote on the local network and send technical logs.
  • Location: some Android versions require this permission to scan for Bluetooth devices. The app does not read or store your location.
  • Notifications and background operation: so recording or streaming continues with the screen off.

5. Legal basis (GDPR)

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)): licence data, without which the app cannot be provided to those who acquired it.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)): technical logs, to keep the app working and fix bugs. You can object to this processing by writing to the contact above.

6. Who we share data with

  • Cloudflare, Inc.: hosts the licence service and sends the sign-in code by email. It acts on our behalf and is covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses for any transfers outside the European Union.
  • YouTube / Google: receives the streams you send to your own channel. That processing is governed by Google's terms and privacy policy, which you accepted when creating the channel.
  • Google Play: if you acquire the app through Google Play, payment is handled by Google. We only receive the order details Google makes available to us.

We do not share data with anyone else, except where required by law.

7. How long we keep data

  • Licence data: while the licence is active and up to 12 months after it expires, to allow renewals. It is then deleted.
  • Technical logs: up to 90 days.
  • Data on the phone: kept until you delete it or uninstall the app.

8. Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction or portability of your data, and object to processing based on legitimate interest. Just write to pjr@ik.me. We reply within one month at the latest.

If you believe your data has not been handled properly, you can lodge a complaint with the Portuguese data protection authority, Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (www.cnpd.pt), or with the authority in your own EU country.

9. Security

Communication with the licence service and the log server is encrypted (HTTPS). Stream keys never leave the phone. Access to licence data and logs is restricted to the controller.

10. Children

The app is not intended for children under 16 and does not knowingly collect children's data. Whoever records or streams a game is responsible for obtaining any required consent from the people filmed, especially from minor athletes and their guardians.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with an updated date. Significant changes are also announced inside the app.
